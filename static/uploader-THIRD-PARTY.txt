VietSub Studio Publish Hub — protocol/selector provenance

The browser flow architecture and selector references were inspected at:
https://github.com/dreammis/social-auto-upload/tree/3847fff01f5434a8a32829833163cd6723cabc59

No upstream runtime or account/cookie data is vendored. The implementation is
independent, fail-closed and mock-tested; upstream/current Bilibili uses biliup CLI,
whereas this app's Bilibili browser flow is independently implemented. Actual form,
privacy, identity and confirmation signals are required, never a click-only success.
